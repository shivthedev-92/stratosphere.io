Stratosphere · Design System v1.0

Night & Dawn

One indigo accent, two atmospheres. Night is the default and keeps the space imagery. Dawn is the light mode: the same sky at sunrise with warm paper surfaces, so it stays calm and modern instead of glossy blue.

Calm by default
One accent per screen. Motion under 250 ms, no bounce.
Guilt-free signals
"Not done" is neutral grey, never red. Red is reserved for destructive actions.
Private by design
Journal text is set in serif, apart from UI chrome; it reads as yours.

Aster, your astronaut coach

default
thinking
celebrating

Aster is a friendly astronaut who has done the long mission and knows big launches happen in small burns. The face lives inside the visor, so moods are just eyes and mouth: thinking while a reply streams, celebrating when a task is marked done. The amber antenna and chest light echo the logo's warm dot.
